Target

CH32V003J4M6

WCH RV32EC core. SW1, SW2, and SW3 are active-low inputs; PA2 / TIM1_CH2N drives the IR output.

Buttons

SW1 > SW2 > SW3

PC4, PC2, and PC1 with internal pull-ups. Confirm package numbering and continuity on the actual board.

Programming

PD1 / SWIO

Remove the battery before connecting a programmer. Use a WCH-LinkE in WCH-LinkRV / RISC-V mode.

Evidence scope

Traced, not supplied

This schematic was reverse-engineered by physically tracing the PCB lines.

It is not a manufacturer-supplied or official TomatoCube schematic. It documents the nets needed to understand the MCU, buttons, IR output, programmer interface, reset, and power connections; it is not fabrication data.

Component tolerances, exact markings, production substitutions, and kit revisions may differ. Check continuity, voltage, polarity, and every pin assignment on your own remote before powering, programming, or building hardware.

Before connecting hardware

Power and programming safety

  • Remove the remote battery before connecting a debugger or programmer.
  • Identify and verify the supply rails, polarity, and common ground.
  • Confirm the schematic nets against the specific board revision.
  • Flash source-backed .bin images at 0x08000000.
  • Hold a remote button during configurable/Samsung Detect or Program operations to keep the six-second SWIO window open.
